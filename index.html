Physics-informed learning for drone dynamics

Prof. Fadi Kurdahi · UC Irvine / Jun 2026 – present

Developing dynamics models on the NeuroBEM dataset for long-horizon trajectory prediction, incorporating physical constraints and trajectory-error penalties into training.

Tail-segment trajectory error on challenging test cases

≈ ⅓ lowerthan a one-step, data-only baseline
≈ ¼ of the errorof a physics-only baseline
Methods & ablations

Evaluated symmetry, monotonicity, and zero-state constraints, individually and in combination. Ablations identified mismatched symmetry assumptions and test-coverage limitations affecting the benefit of these constraints.

Fault diagnosis in Kolmogorov–Arnold Networks

Prof. Fadi Kurdahi · UC Irvine / Jun 2026 – present

Investigating fault localization through single-parameter error injection in a three-person team. Switched from classification to function-fitting tasks to make fault-induced error regions easier to detect.

Observed input-localized error that narrows candidate nodes or layers in function-fitting experiments.

Experimental context

Conducted experiments with regular feedback from senior researchers, compared behavior with MLP baselines, and identified KAN runtime limitations.

Digital circuit design New

4-bit absolute value detector

Prof. Dejan Markovic · UCLA / Sep – Dec 2024

Independently derived a minimal Boolean design from a 128-entry truth table and optimized gate topology and sizing, reaching a minimum delay of approximately 33.7 delay units.

Approximately 60% lower energy (72.91 → 29.165) through joint supply-voltage and gate-sizing optimization, under a 1.5× minimum-delay constraint.
